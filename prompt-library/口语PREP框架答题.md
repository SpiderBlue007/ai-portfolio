# PREP 口语答题框架
P(Point观点): 先直接说出你的核心观点
R(Reason理由): 给出支撑观点的原因
E(Example例子): 举例子来证明
P(Point重述): 最后再次总结观点

## 示例题目：AI makes people stupid.
Point: I don’t agree that AI makes people stupid.
Reason: AI is just a tool. It only helps us save time, instead of replacing our thinking.
Example: When I practice oral English, AI gives me feedback, but I still need to organize ideas by myself.
Point: So AI won’t make people stupid. It depends on how we use it.
